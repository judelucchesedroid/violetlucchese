More than one sitter: a whole team

Violet Care isn’t just me. I have a small network of trusted local friends who babysit. We stay in touch about availability, cover for one another, and work together when a family needs more than one sitter. So even if I’m booked, I can often connect you with someone I know and trust.

  • Birthday parties. We plan, organize, and run creative, super-fun parties with games, crafts, and activities.
  • Parties & gatherings. Enjoy time with your friends while we keep the kids busy, fed, and entertained.
  • School closures & long days. Teacher workdays, school holidays, or longer stretches. Between us, we can often make a schedule work.
